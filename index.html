The market

A grocery that grew a kitchen — and kept the block fed

It started the way the best markets do: shelves of the staples an Ethiopian kitchen can't run without. Then came the seats, the hot trays, the holiday celebrations — and now Kennedy Street has a market where you shop for the week and stay for a plate.
